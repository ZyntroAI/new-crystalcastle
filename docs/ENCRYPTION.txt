## ENCRYPTION.md Summary

**ENCRYPTION.md is an open standard specification (v1.0, 2026) that defines technical encryption implementation standards for AI agents.** It serves as a version-controlled, auditable record of security requirements placed in a repository's root directory.

---

## Core Purpose

The specification addresses a critical gap: without explicit encryption standards, developers make ad-hoc security choices based on convenience rather than correctness. ENCRYPTION.md removes ambiguity by making encryption controls explicit, version-controlled, and compliant with regulatory requirements.

---

## Key Requirements

**Approved algorithms:**
- Symmetric encryption: **AES-256-GCM** and **ChaCha20-Poly1305**
- Hashing: **SHA-256, SHA-512, BLAKE3**

**Forbidden algorithms:**
- DES, RC4 (symmetric)
- MD5, SHA-1 (hashing)

**Key rotation schedules:**
- Data encryption keys: **90-day maximum** (immediate rotation on breach)
- API keys: **30-day maximum**
- TLS certificates: **Annual rotation** (quarterly preferred)

**Infrastructure requirements:**
- **TLS 1.3 minimum** for all new connections
- **4096-bit RSA minimum** for asymmetric encryption
- Approved key storage: environment variables, secrets managers (AWS Secrets Manager, HashiCorp Vault), hardware security modules

---

## Relationship to ENCRYPT.md

ENCRYPT.md defines the **data classification policy** (what is critical, sensitive, internal, or public). ENCRYPTION.md defines the **technical implementation** (which algorithms, TLS versions, and key rotation procedures). Together they provide complete data protection documentation.

---

## Compliance Mapping

ENCRYPTION.md maps to four regulatory standards: **FIPS 140-3, SOC2, ISO 27001, and GDPR** (effective August 2, 2026). It provides the auditable record required by Article 32 of GDPR, SOC2 control CC6.7, ISO 27001 control A.10.1, and the EU AI Act.
